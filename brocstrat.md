### Strategy
**Delegation, not engagement**—replace manual money management with **agentic execution** on our platform: **understand** finances → **find** optimisations → **execute** end-to-end (with approval).

### Why now
Others validated **optimisation** but stalled at **insights**; we are the **execution layer**. Agentic AI + open banking unlock execution; we start with **non-regulated** spend (utilities, broadband), then **regulated** products. Regulators and vendors know the model is broken—inflation, cost, **unsustainable CAC**. Usage compounds a lead competitors can’t backfill.
